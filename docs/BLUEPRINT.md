Known remaining effort changes by the net booking delta, preserving any previously unbooked balance and original estimate. Unknown totals remain null. Removing hours neither reports completion nor refills freed hours elsewhere. Adding hours to waiting work resumes it in the same disclosed operation; removal alone does not.

## September 9: Finish one booked day

- Each booked date has an owner-only **Finish this day** action with an explicit review and **Confirm day finished**. Review shows the selected date, its clock sessions, hours and remaining effort before/after using the authoritative snapshot returned with the preview, including changes made in another tab. - `complete_day { itemId, date }` is a dedicated atomic shared-scheduler command. It subtracts newly completed booked minutes from known remaining effort (floor zero), preserves the original estimate and previously unbooked balance, and leaves unknown effort unknown. Selected daily budgets shrink by those completed minutes; other dates remain unchanged. No completion action books replacement hours automatically.
- The selected sessions retain their original dates, times and protection/reserve metadata, with status recorded as completed. Completion is explicit, never inferred from the clock. Explicit completed reserve use remains consumed even before its old scheduled end, so unrelated ordinary bookings keep their already released capacity.
- Legacy clipped session durations retain their exact stored times. Their summed duration is rounded once to whole minutes for the effort ledger. If an old selected date's remaining quota is no longer a valid 15-minute amount, remove that obsolete dated quota while retaining its residual in overall remaining effort as unbooked work. Other days and historical records remain exact.
- Details group planned and completed hours by date. A finished day shows a checkmark and done hours; remaining days stay editable. Month, week, day and agenda keep completed sessions muted and read-only. Mixed days show done and planned amounts separately; calendar movement selects only planned bookings. ## Architecture contract

`src/lib/types.ts` defines WorkCommand, ScheduleSnapshot, ScheduleProposal, WorkEvent and AppState. Pure TypeScript scheduling is independent of providers. Model tools propose domain commands, never raw database/email operations. All mutation paths revalidate authorization, schedule version and constraints.

Persist committed changes, immutable event and per-recipient notification jobs in one database transaction. Prevent overlap and concurrent stale commits. Retry using stable operation/event IDs. Recompute stale previews; never turn a stale clean-fit request into an authorized displacement. Undo is a version-aware compensating change.

Supabase Queues and Cron run bounded mail workers independently of the browser. Track provider IDs, retries, failures and uncertain outcomes. Resend idempotency lasts 24h; uncertain older sends require reconciliation. Verify webhook signatures and handle repeated/out-of-order callbacks. Keep production credentials server-side.

OpenAI Responses model `gpt-5.6-sol`, medium reasoning, strict schema; `gpt-transcribe` recorded speech. Warn at $20 monthly usage and pause new AI calls at $25 until owner raises allowance. Manual operations remain available. Approximate total operating target $100; Railway is usage-priced, so report observed costs, not a guaranteed fixed total.
